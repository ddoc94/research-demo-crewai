# Boeing Company Profile

## Overview
The Boeing Company develops, manufactures and services commercial airplanes, defense products and space systems for customers in more than 150 countries. It was founded in 1916 and is headquartered in the United States. The company is publicly traded and operates in the Aerospace industry.
