Overview

Novo Nordisk is a Danish public healthcare company founded in 1923. It operates in pharmaceuticals with a focus on diabetes and obesity medications. The company produces diabetes medications including NovoLog and Xultophy, the hemophilia treatment REBINYN, and the obesity treatment Wegovy semaglutide injections.

Independent

Independent
